FestCast manual

Everything the producer needs to run the festival broadcast: from first install to the end of a 9-hour show. Section 15 is a one-page sheet to print for the camera operators.

1. How it fits together

📱 Phones→ VDO.Ninja →OBS (mixes the show)→💾 Recording+📺 TV (wireless or HDMI)
  • FestCast is the control panel. It sets everything up and tells OBS what to do.
  • OBS does the real work: mixing the picture, recording, sending to the TV. If FestCast's window closes, OBS keeps recording.
  • VDO.Ninja carries each phone's video to the PC through a link you send the operator. No app needed.
  • MediaMTX sends the finished show to the TV's web browser over your network.

The dashboard has 8 pages (shortcut keys 1–8): Overview, Cameras, Production, Presentations, Outputs, Recordings, Network, Settings. The top bar always shows OBS status, what's on air (PGM), recording time, and the big STANDBY emergency button.

2. One-time setup

  1. OBS WebSocket: in OBS open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings, tick Enable WebSocket server, keep port 4455, tick Enable Authentication, copy the password, press Apply.
  2. Password into FestCast: in the FestCast folder copy .env.example to .env and set FESTCAST_OBS_PASSWORD= to that password.
  3. OBS video (recommended): Settings → Video: base and output 1920×1080, 30 fps. (FestCast also works with other canvas sizes; 30 fps halves the recording size and the PC load.)
  4. TV output: run powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\get-mediamtx.ps1 once (downloads MediaMTX and checks its checksum).
  5. Start FestCast: double-click start.bat. It picks a free port, prints the addresses and opens the dashboard. Keep that black window open.

Controlling from another laptop/tablet: set FESTCAST_ADMIN_PASSWORD= in .env, restart, and open the "Phones / TVs reach" address on the other device. Without a password only the production PC can control FestCast.

3. The day before

  • Full rehearsal with the real phones, the real TV and the real network (see the checklist in docs/SETUP.md).
  • Optional overnight test: with FestCast running from start.bat, run .venv\Scripts\python scripts\soak_test.py --minutes 540. It runs a 9-hour rehearsal on its own and prints PASS/FAIL plus memory trends, then cleans up.
  • Charge every phone and power bank. Phones need a power bank for a 9-hour show.
  • Windows: Settings → Windows Update → Pause updates. An update restart would end the show.
  • Choose the recording drive: 9 hours of 1080p can need 100–200 GB. Use your biggest drive.
  • Print the camera operator sheet (section 15) and, at the venue, the QR sheet (section 5).

4. Event day: start-up (in this order)

  1. Connect the PC to the festival network (cable if possible). Do this before starting FestCast: phone and TV links use the PC's address on this network.
  2. Turn on Do not disturb; close games, Discord, Steam; plug the PC in.
  3. Open OBS, then double-click start.bat. The top bar must show OBS 33.x, not OBS DOWN.
  4. Cameras → Create production (if not created yet). Enter the event name and the number of cameras.
  5. Send each operator their link or let them scan their QR code (section 5).
  6. Recordings: pick the drive, set Show length to 9, Save, Apply to OBS & verify.
  7. Outputs: open the TV link on the TV, press Start TV output.
  8. Production: build or pick your layout, preview it, TAKE.
  9. Overview → Start production. Recording starts automatically if "auto-start" is on (it is by default).

5. Cameras

Inviting operators

Each camera slot has its own private link. On the Cameras page:

ButtonWhat it does
Copy link / QRThe operator's invitation. Send by chat or let them scan. Contains no passwords.
Print QR sheetOne page with every camera's QR code. Print it at the venue.
+ Add cameraAdds another slot at any time, even mid-show. Other cameras are not touched.
RevokeKills that link and disconnects whoever is using it; a new link is made. Use if a link leaks.

Reading a camera card

StatusMeaning
LiveVideo is arriving. The numbers are what OBS actually receives.
Waiting for phoneReady, but the operator hasn't pressed Start yet.
DisconnectedThe phone stopped sending (screen locked, app switched, Wi-Fi lost). The show is not switched away automatically. After 45 s FestCast also re-opens that camera's viewer by itself.
Not monitoredOBS isn't running this camera's source yet (only right after start-up).

A dash — means "not measured", never zero. A yellow line under a card explains a problem and what to do.

Tally lights on the phones

Every camera phone shows a red border "ON AIR · hold steady" while its picture is on programme (full-screen or in a layout / picture-in-picture), and a green border "PREVIEW · you are next" while it is in preview (Studio Mode). It comes from OBS itself, follows a cut within a moment, and works for local and VDO.Ninja cameras (not for "Open VDO.Ninja directly"). No border = not used; grey "Tally not available" = the phone can't reach FestCast or OBS is down: tell operators to hold steady when unsure.

Camera controls

1080p ▾Quality target. Changing it needs the operator to re-open their link. The card warns if less is received.
Fit / FillFit shows the whole picture (bars if needed); Fill crops to fill the 16:9 frame.
ReconnectRestarts OBS's viewer for that camera (try this first if a picture freezes).
Use audioMakes this phone's microphone live in the show (all phone mics are muted by default).
LiveCuts this camera full-screen to the show (asks to confirm before the production starts).
SettingsName, quality, bitrate and the raw connection statistics.
Disable / RemovePause a slot, or delete it with its OBS source.

6. Switching & layouts (Production page)

Preview → programme

  1. Click a scene button: it goes to PVW (preview, green, not on air).
  2. Check it in the preview monitor.
  3. Press CUT (instant) or TAKE (with the chosen transition, e.g. Fade 500 ms). It is now on PGM (on air, red).

Shortcuts: double-click a scene = straight on air. Direct cut tick-box = single click goes on air.

Layouts (the visual editor)

A layout is a full screen design, for example two cameras side by side with a name banner. Each layout becomes a real OBS scene.

  1. + next to LAYOUTS → name it.
  2. Pick a template (green = suggested for your camera count; there are templates up to 6 cameras and All cameras for any number), or drag cameras from ADD onto the canvas.
  3. Drag to move, pull the white handles to resize (hold Shift to keep the shape). Add Text, Shape, Image / logo or a ready-made Lower third (name banner).
  4. Fine-tune on the right: exact position, crop, fit, colours, lock, hide, layer order.
  5. Changes save and sync to OBS automatically ("Synced to OBS" under the canvas).
  6. Preview then Take live when ready.

Editing a layout that is on air: by default your changes are held. Press Push changes to air when you're happy. Untick the option to edit live directly.

Layouts can be duplicated, exported to a file and imported. Check OBS shows exactly what OBS has.

7. Standby & presentations

Standby is the "we'll be right back" screen: your slideshow, or a branded card with the event name and a clock.

  • STANDBY in the top bar or ACTIVATE STANDBY puts it on air immediately.
  • Return to production goes back to the scene that was on air before (or choose one).
  • Automatic standby (Production → Standby): only when the production is started and no camera is live for the grace period (default 15 s). One camera dropping while another works never triggers it. Returning is manual unless you tick the auto-return option.
  • Scheduled intermissions: add times (e.g. Lunch 12:30–13:15) and standby comes on by itself.

Presentations

  1. Presentations page → drop a PDF, PowerPoint or images. PowerPoint needs LibreOffice installed; otherwise save it as PDF from PowerPoint first. (Animations don't carry over.)
  2. Drag slides to reorder, untick to skip, set seconds per slide, transition, background, branding text.
  3. Preview, then Use for standby.

8. Recording

  • OBS records; FestCast only shows REC when OBS has confirmed it.
  • Files: <your folder>\YYYY-MM-DD\Event_HHMMSS.mkv plus a metadata.json. MKV survives crashes and power cuts (you lose seconds, not the file).
  • Apply to OBS & verify shows what you asked for next to what OBS really uses (green = matches).
  • After each recording the History shows what's really in the file (e.g. h264 1920×1080 aac).
  • Show length: FestCast measures the real recording rate and warns early if the drive would fill before the end.
  • Remux → MP4 converts a finished MKV to MP4 without quality loss (for editing or sharing).

Do not record to a nearly full drive. Rule of thumb: GB needed for 9 h ≈ the bitrate in Mb/s × 4 (e.g. 40 Mb/s → about 160 GB). The Recordings page shows the live bitrate after a minute.

9. TV output

Wireless TV

  1. Outputs → + Wireless TV, give it a name.
  2. On the TV's web browser open its link or scan its QR. (TV with only a Chromecast: open the link in Chrome on a laptop → ⋮ → Cast… → Cast tab.)
  3. Press Start TV output. The picture arrives within seconds. If you see 🔊 Tap for sound, tap once.

The TV reconnects by itself. If the stream ever stops, FestCast restarts it automatically (even after a FestCast crash, the TV keeps playing). Revoke cuts a TV off instantly. To stream to YouTube, Twitch etc. as well, use Live stream below (not OBS's own Stream button: OBS's stream output is busy feeding the TVs).

Live stream (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, any RTMP/SRT server)

  1. Needs ffmpeg once: winget install Gyan.FFmpeg, then restart FestCast.
  2. Outputs → + Destination: pick the platform and paste its stream key (YouTube Studio → Create → Go live → Stream; Twitch → Settings → Stream). Kick / others: paste the server URL too. Add as many as you like.
  3. Press Go live. It starts the TV output if it isn't on: every platform gets the same programme as the TVs. The topbar shows LIVE (amber if a platform is reconnecting).
  4. Check the platform's own page (YouTube Studio's preview) before announcing the stream: FestCast can only see the video leaving this PC (Sending), not what the platform does with it.
  5. After the show: End live stream.

The picture goes out exactly as OBS encodes it: set the bitrate in OBS → Settings → Output → Streaming (YouTube 1080p: 6000-8000 kbps; Twitch: max 6000) and keyframe interval 2 s. Untick a destination to stop just that one. A dropped platform reconnects by itself and touches nothing else. Stream keys are never shown again or written to logs.

HDMI

  1. Connect the TV/projector to the PC by HDMI (Windows sees a second screen).
  2. Outputs → + HDMI projector, choose that screen, Open projector: OBS shows the clean show full-screen there.

Also possible without FestCast: an HDMI splitter, or Windows wireless display (Win+K, choose the TV, set Extend, then use + HDMI projector on that screen).

10. Audio

  • Production → Audio: live meters, mute, volume, sync offset and monitoring for every OBS audio source.
  • Phone microphones are muted by default; choose one with Use audio on its camera card. Nothing switches automatically.
  • A red strip / warning means clipping: lower that source's volume.
  • Use a proper microphone or mixer into the PC for speech and music; phone mics are a backup.

Don't play the show's sound through the production PC's speakers while OBS captures "Desktop Audio": it loops back as an echo or hum. Use headphones for monitoring.

12. When something goes wrong

Golden rule: if in doubt, press STANDBY. The audience sees a clean "we'll be right back" screen while you fix things. Recording continues.

ProblemDo this
A camera shows DisconnectedAsk the operator to unlock the phone and bring the camera page back to the front. Still nothing after a minute: they re-open their link. Then press Reconnect.
Picture sideways / tall with black barsNothing to do for an upright phone: each camera's picture keeps its own shape and is fitted inside its tile (tall picture = tall in the tile). For a bigger upright picture, make the tile tall: Production → layout editor → click the tile → ⟳ Landscape/Portrait. Picture lying on its side: local cameras turn it automatically (the phone asks once for Motion & Orientation: Allow); VDO.Ninja cameras: Turn picture, or the operator turns Portrait Orientation Lock off and re-opens the link.
Picture blurry / below 1080pPhone too far from the router, too hot, or on the front/ultra-wide lens. Move closer or cool it; or set that camera to 720p for a steadier picture.
Phone link says "invalid"It was revoked or expired (48 h). Copy a fresh link from the card.
Phone can't open the link at allPhone not on the same network, or the Wi-Fi blocks devices from seeing each other. Use the production router.
Top bar says OBS DOWNOBS closed or crashed: start OBS again. FestCast reconnects and restores everything by itself.
FestCast window shows "stopped unexpectedly"Nothing: it restarts by itself in 3 s. OBS keeps recording, the TV keeps playing. Reload the dashboard.
Dashboard says "Dashboard offline"Usually the browser lost connection; it reconnects by itself. The show is not affected.
Live stream says ReconnectingRead the message under it (Log for details). A wrong stream key or a stream not set up on the platform usually shows as an I/O error or "connection refused".
TV says "Waiting for the programme"Press Start TV output. Check the MediaMTX log on the Outputs page.
TV picture but no soundTap the TV screen once.
Recording stopped warningCheck the drive (space, unplugged?) and OBS for an error, then press Start recording again. A new file is made; the old one is kept.
Echo or hum from the PC speakersMute the speakers or Desktop Audio (section 10).

Settings → Download support bundle saves the logs (with passwords and links removed) for later investigation.

13. After the show

  1. Return to production or standby for the end card, then stop the recording (Recordings → Stop recording).
  2. Check History says finished and shows the real video format.
  3. Optional: Remux → MP4 for easier sharing.
  4. Outputs → End live stream (if you streamed), then Stop TV output (this also puts OBS's own stream settings back).
  5. Cameras → Close production when you're done for good (invitations stop working; layouts and recordings are kept).
  6. Close the FestCast window, then OBS. Copy the recordings somewhere safe.

15. Camera operator sheet

Print this page for every camera operator.

📱 You are a FestCast camera

Before you start

  1. Join the festival Wi-Fi. Stay close to the router.
  2. iPhone: Control Center → turn Portrait Orientation Lock OFF. Android: Auto-rotate ON.
  3. Settings: Auto-Lock / screen timeout → Never. Low Power Mode OFF. Do not disturb ON.
  4. Plug into a power bank. Keep the phone out of direct sun and hot stage lights.

Go live

  1. Scan your QR code (or open your link) in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap Open camera → Allow the camera.
  3. Choose the rear camera. Microphone: no audio unless the producer asks.
  4. Hold the phone sideways (landscape), check the preview, tap Start.

While filming

  • Never lock the screen or switch apps: the camera stops.
  • Red border = you are ON AIR: hold the shot steady, no reframing. Green border = you are next: get your shot ready now.
  • Move smoothly, hold still on the action, keep it level.
  • If your picture stops: unlock, bring the browser back, or re-open your link.
  • To stop: tap Hang up or close the tab.

16. Glossary

Programme (PGM)What the audience sees and what gets recorded. Red.
Preview (PVW)The next shot, prepared but not on air. Green.
Cut / TakeSwap preview to programme: instantly (Cut) or with a transition (Take).
Layout / sceneA designed screen (cameras + graphics). Each layout is an OBS scene.
StandbyThe "we'll be right back" screen or slideshow.
Lower thirdA name banner in the bottom part of the screen.
Bitrate (Mb/s)How much data per second; higher = better picture, bigger files.
RemuxChange the file container (MKV → MP4) without re-encoding: no quality loss.
MediaMTXThe small local server that sends the show to TV browsers.
VDO.NinjaThe free service that carries phone video to OBS through a web link.
